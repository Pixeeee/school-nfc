
# Privacy Deployment Requirements

Before production, the school must approve:

- Purpose and lawful basis for attendance and SMS processing
- Guardian notice/consent process
- Data minimization and role access
- Retention periods for attendance, SMS logs, audit records, and photos
- Access/correction/export/deletion procedures
- Vendor/data-processing agreements
- Backup and breach-response procedures
- A privacy impact assessment
- Staff training and named incident/privacy contacts

The application deliberately keeps names and phone numbers off NFC cards, masks phone numbers in ordinary UI, limits mobile snapshots, blocks Android backup of the local database, and records sensitive changes. These controls do not replace the school's organizational and legal obligations.
